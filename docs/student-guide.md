# Lightbox student guide

Lightbox is our class's FCPS-II radiology revision space. Everything in it comes from the papers and slides we were given, and every fact was checked by a pipeline before it got here. This guide shows how to get the most out of it.

## Sign in

Your admin gives you a username and a temporary password. At your first sign-in you choose your own password: at least 10 characters, mixing letters with numbers or symbols. You stay signed in for 30 days on each device. If you forget your password, ask the admin to reset it.

To install Lightbox as an app, open it on your phone and choose **Add to Home screen** (Android) or **Share › Add to Home Screen** (iPhone). Pages you have opened stay readable when you lose signal, and you can save whole systems for offline study (see [Study offline](#study-offline)).

## Your first sign-in: three questions

Lightbox asks when your exam is, how much time you have on a normal day and how confident you feel. It uses your answers to set your daily goals, pace your new flashcards and build your plan. You can change them any time with **Change exam date, time or confidence** under the plan on Home.

## Home: today's plan

**Smart today** at the top of Home turns "what should I study?" into one tap:

1. Pick how long you have today: 15, 30 or 60 minutes.
2. Lightbox lists the steps in order, fitted to your time: due flashcards first, then past mistakes to retry, new cards, a short MCQ quiz on a weak area, a topic to read and TOACS stations. Short budgets keep only the first few.
3. Each step opens a ready session. Come back to Home and tick through the rest.

Home also shows:

- **Your streak.** You earn a **streak freeze** (the snowflake) for every 7 days in a row, up to 2. If you miss a day, a freeze covers it and your streak survives. Study the next day to keep it.
- **Readiness.** Your exam readiness today and a projection for exam day if you keep your current pace.
- **This week in class:** the weekly challenge, class polls waiting for your vote and the newest discussion.
- **Mistakes to retry**, **continue where you left off** and any announcements.

## Read the badges

Every fact, and every line of the topic notes, carries a status:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cited** (green) | Checked against a cited source. The link is on the fact page. |
| **Agreed** (cyan) | A descriptive fact where the source and textbook knowledge agree. |
| **Unchecked** (amber) | No source found yet. Don't treat it as verified. |
| **Disputed** (red) | The sources disagree. Both positions are kept. |
| **Edition clash** (violet) | An older text and a newer guideline differ. Both are kept. |

Hover over a badge to see what it means. Open any fact (`/facts/<id>`) to see its source page, its evidence and the original page text.

## Find anything: Ctrl K

Press **Ctrl K** or **/** (or tap the search icon) to search facts, topics, MCQs and image captions from any page. The palette can also jump to any page or start the focus timer.

- **Shorthand works.** Type `RML collapse`, `SUFE` or `DCIS` and Lightbox also searches for "right middle lobe", "slipped upper femoral epiphysis" and "ductal carcinoma in situ".
- **Either spelling works.** `hemorrhage` finds "haemorrhage", and `edema` finds "oedema".
- **Narrow it down.** On a topic page, the chips under the search box limit results to this topic or this system.
- **Typos.** When nothing matches, "Did you mean" suggests the closest words.
- **Fact IDs.** Type an ID like `F-AUG-145` to jump straight to it.

## Keyboard shortcuts

On a computer, press **?** on any page to see every shortcut. The ones that save the most time:

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| `g` then `h`, `s`, `f`, `p`, `a`, `l`, `c` or `t` | Go to Home, Study, Facts, Practice, Analytics, Library, Class or Tutor. |
| `Space`, `1` to `4` | Flashcards: show the answer, then rate Again, Hard, Good or Easy. |
| `A` to `E`, `Enter`, `←` `→` | MCQs: pick an option, check and move on, go back or forward. |
| `R`, `1` to `3`, `Z` | TOACS: reveal, grade yourself, zoom. |

Shortcuts switch off while you're typing in a box, so they never get in the way of a note or an answer.

## Study

- **Study** (`/study`): 183 topic notes grouped into 13 systems. A topic's contents list follows you as you scroll.
- **Compare topics** (`/study/compare`): put two to four topics side by side.
- **Print all** on a system page: printable notes with the statuses written out.
- **Fact explorer** (`/facts`): filter all 463 facts by status, source, kind or system. Your filters are kept in the address, so you can bookmark a view.
- **Atlas** (`/atlas`): 69 images with their exact source captions. Tap an image to zoom.
- **Focus mode** (the expand icon in the top bar) hides the navigation so you can read.

### Tools in a topic note

The buttons at the top of every topic note help you learn it, not just read it:

- **Mastery dots** next to each point show how well you recall its flashcard: strong, learning or lapsed.
- **Learn this topic** starts a flashcard session with just this topic's cards.
- **Self-test** blurs every point. Try to recall it, then tap it to check.
- **Read aloud** reads the note to you in your browser's voice and highlights the point being read.
- **Quick check** at the end asks a few recall cards and the past-paper questions from the same pages.
- **AI study aids:** a 60-second recap, memory hooks and how the topic is examined. Each one is made once from the topic's facts and shared with the class.
- Lightbox remembers where you stopped and brings you back there next time.

### Fix a weak topic

When a topic keeps tripping you up, **Fix it** (on Analytics, in the note itself or on a forgotten flashcard) opens four short steps at `/fix/<topic>`:

1. Re-read the key points.
2. Drill 10 of the topic's flashcards.
3. Answer three questions.
4. **Is it fixed?** Get 2 of the 3 right and your older slips on this topic stop counting against you.

### Study offline

On a system page (for example `/study/chest`), press **Save offline**. Lightbox saves every topic in that system, its print view and the images it needs onto your device. The button counts up while it saves, then shows **Saved offline**. Those notes now open with no signal at all.

See and remove your saved systems, with their sizes, under **Account › Offline packs**. Signing out removes them.

## Flashcards and FSRS

Open **Practice › Flashcards** (`/practice/cards`). The 420 cards come from verified facts. Lightbox schedules them with **FSRS**, which works out the best moment to show each card again, just before you would forget it.

1. Read the front, try to recall the answer, then tap the card or press **Space** to flip it.
2. Rate how it went:

   | Rating | Key | Use it when |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Again** | 1 | You didn't know it. |
   | **Hard** | 2 | You recalled it with effort. |
   | **Good** | 3 | You recalled it normally. Pressing Space again also rates Good. |
   | **Easy** | 4 | It was instant. |

   On a phone you can also swipe: right for Good, left for Again.
3. Be honest. Pressing Again isn't a failure. It brings the card back sooner, which is exactly what you want.

You get **20 new cards a day**, plus whatever is due. Once you set your exam date, Lightbox paces new cards instead, so that the whole deck is learned about two weeks before the exam and the last stretch is reviews only. The Flashcards page tells you whether you're on track.

Cards you fail come back within the same session. Reviews save even when you're offline and sync when you reconnect. Flag a card as a **weak spot** to drill it later. A card you forget 4 times becomes a **leech**: it's marked weak automatically and offers **Fix this topic**.

Two more settings help:

- **How much to remember** (**Account › Study settings**): your target recall, from 80% to 97%. Higher means more reviews. In the last 14 days before your exam it rises to at least 93% on its own.
- **Read aloud:** the speaker icon on the flashcard page reads each question and then its answer, so you can revise hands-free.

## MCQs, quizzes and timed exams

- **MCQ bank** (`/practice/mcq`): past-paper questions, answered blind. Before you check, say how sure you are: **Sure**, **Unsure** or **Guess**. After you answer, you see the key, the verdict and the evidence. Disputed keys are flagged, and questions with no key in the source are marked as unscored. Analytics compares your confidence with your accuracy, so you learn where you're overconfident.
- **Why B, not C?** After a wrong answer, ask the AI why the key beats the option you picked. It answers from the facts on that question's page and cites them.
- **Mistake bank:** every wrong answer, and every right answer you only guessed, joins your mistakes. They come back after 1, 3, 7 and 21 days until you get them right. Retry them from Home or with the **Mistakes due** set in the MCQ bank.
- **Build a quiz** (`/practice/builder`): pick a ready-made set, such as a **Full paper** or **Quick 10**, or filter by paper, system, key status and your own history (for example, only the ones you got wrong). In **Practice** mode you check each answer as you go.
- **Timed exam** (`/practice/builder?mode=exam`): set a time per question or for the whole paper. Answers save as you go. You can flag questions and jump around with the question grid, and the key only appears after you submit. The results page reviews every question, and you can retake the same paper.

## TOACS stations

**Practice › TOACS** (`/practice/toacs`) runs timed image stations of 30, 60, 90 or 120 seconds.

1. Look at the image. Press **Z** to zoom.
2. Type your answer in the box. It stays private and is never saved.
3. Compare your answer with the model answer. The model answer comes only from the source caption and the facts on the same page, each with its status. Lightbox adds no diagnosis of its own. If you like, ask the **AI coach** to mark your answer against those facts and suggest a grade.
4. Grade yourself **Got it**, **Partly** or **Missed**. You always choose the grade. Partly counts as half, and you can repeat the stations you missed.

## The highlighter

Select any text in a topic note and a toolbar appears:

- **Four highlight colours.** Your highlights come back every time you open the page.
- **Note:** pin a private note to the highlight.
- **Make a flashcard:** opens a card prefilled from the verified fact. Your card joins your daily reviews.
- **Copy with source:** copies the text with the page title, its fact ID and source page, and a link back.
- **Search:** searches Lightbox for the selection.

Tap an existing highlight to recolour it, edit its note or delete it. Keyboard users can press **Tab** right after selecting to reach the toolbar.

## Your library

**Library** (`/library`) collects everything you've saved. Only you can see it:

- **Bookmarks** and **Weak spots:** set with the bookmark and flag icons on facts, topics, MCQs, images and cards.
- **Collections:** group items around a revision theme. Share a collection with the class when it's ready. Classmates' shared collections are under **Class collections**, where you can copy them into your own library.
- **Notes:** your private notes, with **Highlights** grouped by page. **Turn highlights into flashcards** puts every highlighted fact on a spaced schedule in one tap.
- **My cards:** the flashcards you made. You can edit or delete them here.
- **History:** what you read, answered and reviewed over the last 30 days.
- **My high-yield sheet** (`/library/high-yield`): a one-page revision sheet built from what *you* keep missing: lapsed cards, Again ratings, wrong answers and weak marks from the last 90 days. Weak topics come first, worst first, and every line keeps its status and fact IDs. It changes as you improve. Print it, or save it as a PDF for the night before the exam.

## Export to Anki

You can take Lightbox cards into Anki, AnkiDroid or AnkiMobile:

- **Library › Export to Anki:** choose all verified cards, one system, your bookmarks, your weak spots, your own cards or one of your collections. You'll see how many cards are in your choice before you download.
- **On a collection page:** **Export to Anki**.
- **On a system page** (for example `/study/chest`): **Anki deck**.

Pick **Anki deck (.apkg)** or **Spreadsheet (.csv)**, then download.

- **Anki desktop:** choose **File › Import** and pick the `.apkg` file.
- **AnkiDroid or AnkiMobile:** open the downloaded file and share it to Anki.

The deck is called `Lightbox::<your choice>`. Each card shows its source and status under the answer. If you import a newer export later, Anki updates the cards you already have instead of adding duplicates. Your own cards are labelled as not verified.

To keep a copy of everything else, go to **Account › Download my data**. You get one JSON file with your bookmarks, collections, notes, highlights, cards, goals, exams, answers, reviews, focus sessions, comments, reports and AI tutor chats.

## Focus timer and goals

The timer pill sits in the bottom corner of every page.

- **Sessions:** start a **25/5** or **50/10** Pomodoro. It keeps running as you move between pages. You can show the countdown in the tab title or play a soft chime when a phase ends.
- **Daily goals:** set them in the timer panel. There are goals for cards, MCQs and focus minutes, plus an optional exam date. Home shows your progress against them.
- **Daily reminder** (**Account › Study settings**): one notification a day, at the time you choose, with your due cards and mistakes. It's skipped on days you've already cleared them. On iPhone, add Lightbox to your Home Screen first.
- **Analytics** (`/analytics`): your activity heatmap, mastery by system, accuracy trend, forgetting curve, confidence calibration, exam readiness with an exam-day projection, and weakest topics, each with a **Fix it** button.

## The AI tutor

**Tutor** (`/tutor`) answers questions using only Lightbox facts. AI also appears as **Explain** on fact pages, **Why B, not C?** after a wrong MCQ, study aids in topic notes, the TOACS coach and **Generate MCQs** in topic notes.

- **Grounded:** answers cite the fact IDs they used. Open them and check. If Lightbox doesn't cover something, the tutor says so instead of guessing.
- **Grounding badge:** each answer shows whether it stood on cited facts, said the notes don't cover it, or neither. Treat the last kind with extra care.
- **Next steps:** under the latest tutor answer, tap a follow-up question, **Quiz me** on the facts it cited, or **Learn these cards** to add their verified flashcards to your reviews.
- **Thumbs up or down:** tell the admins which answers helped. They review the votes.
- **Not verified:** AI text is always labelled as AI. Treat it as a study partner's explanation, not as verified content.
- **Daily limit:** each person has a daily limit, and repeated questions are answered from a cache.
- **Practice MCQs:** generated MCQs only reach **Practice › AI MCQs** (`/practice/ai`) after an admin has reviewed them.

## Class

**Class** (`/class`) is where we help each other:

- Discuss facts, MCQs and disputed items in comment threads, and upvote good explanations.
- Vote in class polls on disputed keys.
- See the leaderboard.
- **Weekly challenge:** every Monday, everyone gets the same 15 keyed past-paper questions, one minute each. You get one attempt, and the 15-minute clock keeps running if you leave the page, so do it in one sitting. The week's board ranks everyone who finished by score, then by speed.
- **Where the class slips:** the questions the class gets wrong most, with the most popular wrong answer, and the topics where people most often forget cards or answer wrong, over the last 30 days. It's anonymous: no names, and nothing appears until at least 3 people have answered.

Use **Report** on a fact or topic when something looks wrong. Reports go to the admins and on to the pipeline, which owns the content. Lightbox itself never changes a fact because of a comment or a vote.

Questions or problems? Ask your admin.
